About this extension

YouTube AI Summarizer is a Chrome extension that reads captions for the video you are watching and helps you generate summaries, follow-up chat grounded in the transcript, and optional podcast-style audio — using providers you configure (for example Groq or Ollama Cloud) or optional managed AI where available.

Your API keys and most preferences stay in the browser unless you choose features that explicitly use sign-in or cloud processing described in our privacy policy. We do not run ads or analytics telemetry in the extension.
