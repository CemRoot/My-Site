About this extension

Gleano is a Chrome extension that summarizes YouTube videos (from captions) and web articles (via Article Reader on the tab you open). Get summaries, transcript- or article-grounded chat, and optional podcast-style audio — using providers you configure (for example Groq or Ollama Cloud) or optional managed AI where available.

Your API keys and most preferences stay in the browser unless you choose features that explicitly use sign-in or cloud processing described in our privacy policy. We do not run ads or analytics telemetry in the extension.
